# Host guide: playing THE SYNDICATE with friends

1. **Open the site and press "Host a game".** Pick a nickname and avatar. Leave beginner mode on
   for a first game.
2. **Point new players at the guide.** The homepage has a **How to play** button (or share
   `<site>/how-to-play`); inside a game, **Rules** links to it too.
3. **Share the room.** Read out the 6-character code, press "Share invite link", or let people
   scan the QR code. Everyone plays on their own phone or computer, from any network.
4. **Wait for 4-12 players.** Everyone presses "I'm ready". You can change match length, pace and
   chat style, and remove anyone who joined by mistake. Then press "Start match".
5. **Each operation has three steps.** Study the operation and your private intel; negotiate
   (chat and deals are not enforced); seal 0-3 credits. Then everyone sees the outcome and who
   kept their promises. Phases end on a timer, or earlier when everyone is done.
6. **If someone drops out,** the game carries on: their commitment counts as 0 until they return.
   They get their seat back by opening the same room on the same device (or tapping the room
   link again). Only one tab per seat is active at a time.
7. **At the end,** the final reckoning shows scores, highlights and exactly why each operation
   succeeded or failed. Press "Rematch" to play again with the same group.

Good to know: credits are fictional; there are no accounts, ads or purchases. As host you can mute
a player or switch the room to preset-only chat, but you can never see anyone's secrets.
